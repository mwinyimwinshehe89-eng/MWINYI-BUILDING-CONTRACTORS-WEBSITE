ABOUT MBC

Local understanding.
Lasting work.

MWINYI BUILDING CONTRACTORS COMPANY LTD is based in Zanzibar, Tanzania. We provide building, renovation, and project support for homes, businesses, and government projects.

We work with clients to understand their needs, plan the work, and create practical spaces that last. Good communication, careful work, and respect for agreed timelines are important on every project. Time and quality are our priorities.
